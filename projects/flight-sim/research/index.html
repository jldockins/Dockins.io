Research

Things looked up while building this, with sources. Confidence is marked honestly — verified means read in a primary document, unverified means it is a working assumption and should be treated as one.

Certificate credit — an uncertified home sim earns none

verified14 CFR 61.51(g); FAA AC 61-136B · looked up 2026-09-01

Part 61 credit attaches to an FAA-approved device only — a full flight simulator, a flight training device (FTD), or an aviation training device (ATD, basic/BATD or advanced/AATD). Approval is device-specific, carried by a Letter of Authorization valid five years. A consumer setup running X-Plane or MSFS earns nothing toward a certificate unless that specific hardware configuration has been qualified. Bearing on this project: This rig will not be an approved device. Hours in it are worth zero toward any rating. That is a fact about the regulation, not a defect in the build — but it removes "hours toward the instrument rating" as an available goal.

Instrument currency — the one case needing no instructor

verified14 CFR 61.57(c); 61.51(g)(5) · looked up 2026-09-01

Instrument currency is six approaches, holding procedures, and intercepting and tracking courses within the preceding six calendar months, completable in any combination of aircraft, FFS, FTD or ATD. Since the 2018 rule change the device-based portion needs no instructor sign-off — but the device must still be approved, and the logbook must identify device, time and content. Separately, 61.57(d): an IPC cannot be done in an ATD at all, only an aircraft, FFS or FTD. So an ATD maintains currency but cannot restore it once lapsed past twelve months. Bearing on this project: The nearest thing to a certifiable use case here, and still out of reach without approval. Practically, currency has to be earned in the airplane.

Logged training still requires an instructor present

verified14 CFR 61.51(g)(4), 61.51(h) · looked up 2026-09-01

Instrument aeronautical experience in a simulator or training device counts only with an authorized instructor present to observe the session, signing the logbook or training record. Solo practice in a device is not loggable training regardless of the device. Bearing on this project: Even an approved device would not turn solo evening practice into logged time. The value here is proficiency, not paperwork.
